Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction & Authorized Independent Real Estate Consultancy Identity

Welcome to properties-s.com. We operate as an independent real estate consultancy and marketing platform based in Egypt, conducting digital marketing campaigns on behalf of leading property developers, including SODIC (Sixth of October Development & Investment Company).

properties-s.com is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our website visitors and prospective clients. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, process, share, and safeguard your personal information when you browse our landing pages, submit inquiries, or interact with our real estate advisory services in compliance with international advertising standards, Google Ads policies, and applicable data privacy regulations.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or submit an interest form, we collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us:

  • Contact Identification: Full Name, Primary Phone Number, Country Code, and WhatsApp contact details.
  • Property Preferences: Project interests (e.g., The Lakes at SODIC East, Ogami Ras El Hekma, East Vale Mostakbal City), unit type preferences, and budget parameters.
  • Inquiry Notes & Communications: Records of communications, questions, or requests made via form submissions, phone conversations, or WhatsApp chats.
  • Technical & Analytical Data: IP address, device type, browser specifications, operating system, pages viewed, time spent on pages, referring URLs, and campaign identifiers captured automatically via cookies and web analytics tags.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate commercial independent real estate consultancy and advisory operations:

  • To contact you directly via phone, SMS, WhatsApp, or email to respond to your registered interest.
  • To furnish official developer brochures, master plans, pricing lists, payment schedules, and priority reservation guidance.
  • To facilitate private consultations, virtual meetings, or scheduled on-site visits with senior property advisors.
  • To verify lead authenticity and optimize the quality and relevance of our real estate advertising campaigns.

4. Information Sharing & Developer Partnership Disclosure

We respect your privacy: We do NOT sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers or unaffiliated advertisers.

Your information is shared strictly with authorized real estate developer partners (specifically SODIC Developments) and licensed sales consultants exclusively to the extent necessary to verify pricing, unit availability, and complete sales advisory or contract booking processes requested by you.

5. Cookies, Google Ads & Third-Party Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies, tracking pixels, and related technologies to ensure proper website functionality, evaluate marketing performance, and measure conversion effectiveness:

  • Google Ads & Google Analytics: We utilize Google advertising tools, including conversion measurement and analytics tags. These tools place cookies on your device to analyze how users navigate our site, measure lead conversion actions, and deliver relevant advertisements based on past visits.
  • Third-Party Vendors: Third-party vendors, including Google, use first-party and third-party cookies together to inform, optimize, and serve advertisements across the internet.

6. Your Choices & Opt-Out Instructions

You have complete control over how cookies and advertising tags track your online activity:

  • Google Ads Personalization: You can customize or opt out of Google's personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings page.
  • Industry Opt-Out: You may opt out of third-party behavioral advertising cookies through the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) Opt-Out Page or the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA).
  • Browser Controls: You can configure your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox) to block or delete cookies at any time.

7. Data Security & Retention

properties-s.com implements robust technical, physical, and administrative safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of your personal data.
